## Design Specificity Verdict

**Authored, unmistakably.** Butter-yellow ground, aqua accent, black mono pills, the `.rule-open` "line opening" motif, and a distinctive copy voice ("Skip the calling. Keep the deciding."). Passes the AI-slop checklist. The console layout's blob field flirts with DESIGN.md's forbidden gradient blobs but survives subordinated behind glass.

**Deterministic scan:** static scan of `app/` + `components/` clean (exit 0). Runtime scan found 75 findings across 5 pages, dominated by: an app-shell cluster on every console page (7.5px "ALPHA", 10.5px "Pipeline", 9.5px "Powered by", one 11px body hit, one nested-card — all TopBar/Sidebar chrome, one fix clears five pages); line-length 91–171 chars/line on job and candidate detail prose; one real contrast failure — 4.2:1, #55793b on #e9efc8 (green badge on job detail). False positives: 6× "ai-color-palette" cyan-gradient hits on the landing are the deliberate brand aqua; 2× clipped-overflow likely intentional decorative containers.

**Visual overlays:** injection succeeded; overlays were left visible in the "[Human]" tab on the candidate detail page.

## Overall Impression

A genuinely authored product with a world-class safety-legibility story (the script-as-artifact panel, named+costed confirms) undermined by a handful of Operate-surface ergonomics: the live-status chip that doesn't know the truth, a flat CTA hierarchy that makes the costliest action look like a form submit, and chrome typography below legibility floors. The single biggest opportunity: make the console's *status and hierarchy* as honest as its copy already is.

## What's Working

1. **The script-as-artifact panel** (`calls/[id]/page.tsx:327`) — rendering the literal string sent to CALL-E with guard violations highlighted in place converts the safety model from claim to visible object. The demo's money shot.
2. **Named, costed confirmation** (`CallButton.tsx`) — "Yes, call Asha" + "uses one CALL-E call" survives the user forgetting which row they clicked.
3. **Copy as design material** — the eyebrow/mono machine-label + human-sentence register is the most distinctive system after the palette.

## Priority Issues

- **[P1] The "Calls are live" chip lies by default.** `Sidebar.tsx:120–151` renders the red live chip unconditionally; `lib/config.ts` knows `liveCallsEnabled` but the UI never asks. Against the fake server the console still swears it can ring people — inverting the product's own #1 rule. **Fix:** pass config into the layout; red "Calls are live" only when armed, neutral "Offline — calls simulated" otherwise. Suggested: /impeccable harden.
- **[P1] CTA hierarchy is flat at the moment it matters most.** Black pill = "New job", "Upload resumes", "Create job", "Place call", and 19 row-level "Call" buttons. The costliest action is visually interchangeable with a form submit. **Fix:** demote row-level Call to soft variant; one black pill per view. Suggested: /impeccable layout.
- **[P2] Accessibility cluster.** Disabled-reason via `title` only (`RowActions.tsx:147`), score meaning in hover tooltips, confirm-swap drops keyboard focus to body at the dial moment, sidebar hover via `onMouseEnter` with no `:focus` twin, plus detector's 4.2:1 green-badge contrast and 7.5–10.5px chrome text. **Fix:** visible microcopy for reasons, focus moved to "Yes, call X" on reveal, chrome text ≥11px, badge palette to 4.5:1. Suggested: /impeccable audit → harden.
- **[P2] The match score column is a naked number.** `jobs/[id]/page.tsx:233–263` — "91" unlabeled; neither keyboard users nor a cold judge get its meaning. **Fix:** mono microlabel ("MATCH") in header row. Suggested: /impeccable clarify.
- **[P2] No narrow framing exists.** Fixed 248px sidebar, fixed row columns, `whiteSpace: nowrap`, zero media queries in the console; detail-page prose runs 91–171 chars/line (detector-confirmed). A half-screen laptop breaks the queue. **Fix:** sidebar collapses under ~1000px, queue actions wrap, prose max-width ~70ch. Suggested: /impeccable adapt.

## Persona Red Flags

**Alex (recruiter working the queue):** 19 one-at-a-time "Build script" clicks before the promise is armed — no "Build all scripts"; `/profiles` has 51 people with no search/sort/filter; after confirming a call the row scrolls away with no toast/anchor; "Move to…" select sits one misclick from de-shortlisting mid-scan.

**Sam (screen reader + keyboard):** disabled-reason `title` span announced as nothing; score tooltips pointer-only; confirm swap drops focus to body right before dialing a human; sidebar hover states have no focus equivalent. Credit: real buttons/links, `aria-current` set, good global `:focus-visible`.

**Jordan (hackathon judge, cold):** landing → "Open the console" → a nearly empty `/jobs` with one card; the strongest screens are three clicks deep with no signpost; "Profiles" nav vs "/candidates" pages breaks the mental map; "Screening Calls" lists rows that were never calls ("Script ready").

## Questions to Consider

1. Should the interface remember a human's "no"? Voicemail and "please don't call me" both resolve to the same "Call again" affordance.
2. The one-by-one gate is a safety feature for dialing — why is it also applied to script building, which is free?
3. Where does the money show? "Uses one CALL-E call" appears only in confirm microcopy; no running count anywhere.
